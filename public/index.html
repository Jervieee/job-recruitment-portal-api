Why JobHunters?

Inspired by the spirit of adventure and teamwork of the Grand Line, JobHunters was engineered as a full-stack Express.js + MongoDB recruitment platform. We believe job hunting should never feel like sailing blind—that's why job browsing is protected for registered members, companies are verified by platform admins, and every application preserves a clear status history timeline.
