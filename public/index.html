Why JobHunters PH?

Inspired by the spirit of adventure and camaraderie of the Grand Line, JobHunters PH was engineered as a full-stack Express.js + MongoDB recruitment platform. We keep job browsing exclusive to registered members, verify hiring companies through platform admins, and give every candidate a transparent 4-stage status timeline from submission to offer.
